# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Indonesian MSME (*Usaha Mikro, Kecil, dan Menengah* / UMKM) founders, business owners, and operators looking to assess their enterprise's health index, identify critical operational vulnerabilities, and prepare for formal mentoring, licensing, and capital scaling.

## Product Purpose

The platform functions as an AI-powered Enterprise Diagnostic & Health Check Tool built in collaboration with **OK OCE Indonesia** and **USCM**. It automates comprehensive 5-pillar operational audits (Financials, Operations/SOP, Marketing, HR, Legality), assigns formal classification under Indonesian MSME Law (*UU UMKM No. 20/2008 & PP No. 7/2021*), provides tactical 30-day turnaround plans, and routes entrepreneurs into tailored mentorship stages mapped directly to the official **7 TOP OK OCE Curriculum** (*Pendaftaran, Pelatihan, Pendampingan, Perizinan, Pemasaran, Pelaporan Keuangan, Permodalan*).

## Positioning

Unlike generic AI business plan generators or static questionnaires, this tool delivers an authoritative diagnostic engine deeply contextualized in Indonesian regulatory frameworks (*UU UMKM*), provides actionable P0/P1/P2 vulnerability rankings, and features an interactive in-app AI Mentor acting as a dedicated 1-on-1 coach with direct WhatsApp escalations to verified OK OCE mentors.

## Operating Context

- Used by founders on desktop and mobile web during self-assessments or guided intake sessions with OK OCE mentors.
- Produces printable and exportable executive diagnostic summaries (PDF) for bank loan discussions (KUR), investor meetings, and offline mentoring reviews.
- Integrates live interactive chat with Google Gemini for contextualized coaching.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Multi-step Diagnostic Wizard**: 5-pillar input collection with built-in instant presets (*Kopi Nusantara*, *Batik Kreasi*, *AutoTech*).
- **AI Diagnostic Engine**: Real-time evaluation via Google Gemini REST API generating 0–100 health indices, 6-axis pillar radar data, and 12-month simulation forecasts.
- **UU UMKM Categorization**: Automatic rule-based tier assignment (Usaha Mikro, Usaha Kecil, Usaha Menengah, Usaha Besar).
- **Interactive AI Mentoring Assistant**: In-app conversational coach retaining full diagnostic context.
- **Tactical Toolkit**: Generates standard SOP templates, cash flow management rulebooks, and financing pitch summaries.
- **Constraints**: Pure light theme interface adhering to official brand color identities (USC Cardinal Red `#990000` & Gold `#FFCC00`, OK OCE Blue `#0284c7`, and crisp white backgrounds).

## Brand Commitments

- **OK OCE Indonesia**: Social movement for job creation & MSME advancement. Official logos and 7 TOP curriculum stages must be strictly honored.
- **USCM Partner Identity**: Cardinal Red and Gold accents paired with OK OCE visual language.
- **Tone & Voice**: Empowering, pragmatic, highly actionable, respectful of Indonesian entrepreneurial realities.

## Product Principles

1. **Pragmatism Over Jargon**: Deliver concrete 14-day and 30-day actions rather than theoretical advice.
2. **Actionable Vulnerability Visibility**: Highlight leaks and red flags with clear severity, estimated resolution time, and expected ROI.
3. **Seamless Mentorship Continuity**: Bridge the gap between automated digital diagnosis and human/AI mentorship.
4. **Legitimacy & Trust**: Uphold official Indonesian legal standards and partner credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Clean high-contrast typography and clear badge indicators for all MSME scales.
- Responsive layout supporting mobile and desktop workflows for diverse operational environments across Indonesia.
